Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-04

This policy explains how Jigsaw Puzzles, a mobile application published by IceCloud Games, handles information. In short: your photos, your puzzle progress, and your purchases never leave your device.

01 What We Collect (and Don't)

Your personal content — the photos you pick and the progress you make on each puzzle — is stored entirely on your own device. We do not collect it, upload it, or look at it in any way.

The app does use Google Analytics for Firebase to gather anonymous usage statistics, such as which screens are visited and which features are tapped. This helps us understand how the app is used and where it can be improved. None of it includes your photos or puzzle content. See the third-party policies below for details.

02 Device Permissions

To deliver its core features, the app may request the following permissions — each used strictly for the purpose described:

  • Media and gallery access: lets you choose pictures from your own gallery to turn into puzzles. Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server.
  • Internet access: used to download puzzle images from our storage and to display ads through Google AdMob. No personal data is sent to us during this process.
  • Notifications: used for local reminders about the new daily puzzle. Every notification is scheduled and generated on your device — we run no push server and collect nothing through this feature. You can turn notifications off anytime in system settings.

03 Purchases and Subscriptions

The app offers an optional VIP membership subscription. All billing is handled exclusively by Google Play — we never see, store, or process your payment details or any financial information.

Your subscription status (whether you are currently a VIP member) is kept in a secure local database on your device and is not sent to our servers.

04 Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party services, which may collect certain information as described in their own policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob & Advertising Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Analytics for Firebase

05 Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

06 Updates to This Policy

We may revise this policy from time to time, so it's worth checking this page occasionally. Any change takes effect as soon as it is posted here.
